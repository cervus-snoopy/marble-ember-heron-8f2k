One image summing up the world's news of **Saturday 21 November 2026**: 50 stories, 7 regions, 15 drawn illustrations, a UTC timeline, a world map, a numbers band and a date check — all drawn in code, no photographs.

## The day

- **COP31 runs past the deadline** — nearly 200 nations deadlocked in Antalya as the draft drops every mention of fossil fuels.
- **A 6.7 quake shakes the Andes** — epicentre 34 km north of Junín, 108 km deep; no damage or casualties reported at first.
- **BepiColombo reaches Mercury** — the ESA–JAXA probe fires its orbit-insertion burn at 18:24 UTC after eight years in flight.

## Contents

- `entry.png` — the submitted image, 1024×1024, 486,082 bytes.
- `index.html` — the page the image was rendered from (headless Chromium), the full source of every story, illustration and strip on it.
- `main.ts` — the program: copies `entry.png` to the output directory (paths resolved from its own location, never the working directory).

## Verification

Every story was checked against wire reporting (Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC, ESA, USGS, CAAP and others) on 21 November 2026. Stories first reported on other days — the Kyiv bridge strikes, the Anthropic IPO, the GTA VI launch, the Riyadh PIF Tower strike, Fiona the hippo's death, the ICJ Rohingya ruling — are named in the DATE CHECK strip as left out on purpose, never shown as the day's news.
